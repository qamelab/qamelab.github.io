# Signal

Long-form pieces by the comms team's S. Ignal persona, exported
automatically by qame-comms on editorial approval — do not hand-edit
exported files; fix the piece in the qame-comms dashboard and re-export.

## Filename

`YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. The filename is canonical — it becomes the URL
slug at `/signal/<slug>/`.

## Frontmatter

Same shape as reviews (see `src/content/reviews/_README.md`): `date` +
`title` required; `summary`, `byline`, and the optional
`targetOutlet` / `targetUrl` / `targetHeadline` describing the piece
under discussion (absent on producer-mode analyses).
